# Submission checklist
Group deliverables
- [ ] Digital project (zip the whole folder, including `assets/`)
- [ ] Online demonstration, 7-10 minutes, with all six parts: 1 introduction to the work, 2 brief literary analysis, 3 contemporary issue, 4 connection between work and issue, 5 demonstration of the project, 6 conclusion (see the demonstration walkthrough in README.md; the private shortcut in README can show the ending quickly)
- [ ] Narrative report, 5-8 pages (excluding references, header/footer, screenshots): introduction; literary analysis; digital-age issue; connection; description of the project; group members' roles; conclusion; references
- [ ] Member names, roles and actual contributions entered in `MEMBERS` (data.js); photos added at `assets/member-1/2/3.png`
- [ ] Open every Source Register link (especially those listed as unchecked in README), confirm it matches the entry, and fix or replace any that do not
- [ ] Add page numbers for any claim you take from a book or article; add novel quotations only from your own copy, with edition and page
- [ ] Decide whether to add a scholarly source for the strongest historical claims
- [ ] Your own radio track at `assets/audio/radio-track.mp3`, and the Doro files (`doro-attacks-tokyo.jpg`, `doro-article.png` optional, `doro-truth.mp4`), only if you have the right to use them
- [ ] Disclose the AI assistance as your course requires
- [ ] Reset progress (Menu > Reset) before recording the demonstration
Individual (separate from the website)
- [ ] Peer evaluation, submitted directly to the instructor (confidential, 1-5 ratings)
- [ ] Self-reflection
## About reference status (for the group's own use)
If you add a new source later: **Verification pending** means you have not yet opened the link and checked it supports the claim; **Citation incomplete** means details (edition, publisher, year, pages) are missing; **Verified** means you checked it; **Project original** means the group made it. Only call something verified after checking it. If a link fails or does not support the claim, correct or replace it.
